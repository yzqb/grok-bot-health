Privacy Policy

Grok Bot Health is used only by its owner to read the owner's own Google Health data with read-only permissions. Data is stored privately on the owner's own computer, is never sold, shared, published, or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
